About Us

A Farmer-Owned Manufacturing Unit

Neem India Traders was founded in Kokkarayanpettai, Namakkal, Tamil Nadu, on the belief that organic products should come straight from the people who grow them — no middlemen, no compromise.

Since 2019, we've cold-pressed our neem, coconut, groundnut and sesame oils in-house — the same way local farming families have for generations, refined for modern quality standards.

Every product is dried, pressed, tested and packed under one roof, so what leaves our unit is exactly what reaches your home.

FAQ

Good to Know

Is the neem oil 100% pure?

Yes — every bottle is cold-pressed from neem seeds sourced directly from our own and partner farms, with no dilution or additives.

Do you deliver across India?

Yes, regular deliveries run all over India by road, air, and sea depending on your location and order size.

Can I place a bulk order for farming use?

Absolutely — reach out to us directly and we'll arrange bulk pricing for neem kernel powder and oil cake for agricultural use.

What's the difference between neem cake and neem kernel powder?

Neem oil cake is the residue left after oil extraction, used as a soil enricher and insecticide. Neem kernel powder is made from whole dried seeds and used more broadly in organic pest control.

How do I contact you after ordering?

You can email neemindiatraders@gmail.com or call +91 99445 22706 any time — we personally handle every order.
